# Provider onboarding protocol

Provider outreach is sent from the deployment operator's normal organizational identity. The
message states the concrete purpose: On the Line checks public community-service listings, applies only
explicit routine changes, and routes removals and judgment calls to a curator.

The initial conversation asks the provider to:

- identify the public listing fields that change most often;
- share organization-and-service records in Open Referral HSDS 3.2, excluding client data;
- nominate one organizational business line for verification;
- designate an authorized representative to define permitted days, local hours, validity dates,
  and maximum call count;
- choose whether the organization is named or anonymized in published project material;
- approve any quotation before it is attributed.

Before onboarding, the operator sends `consent-form.md`. A curator imports the HSDS record, creates
the consent in On the Line with the signed document reference, and confirms the resulting audit event.
The first call is scheduled inside the agreed window. The provider receives the verified HSDS
record and its evidence trail after the call.

Withdrawal is accepted through the provider's established contact channel or verbally during any
call. The curator records withdrawal immediately. A verbal stop request also ends the active call
and permanently suppresses the organization in the same verification transaction.
